The AI Engine

See the difference between a promise and a maybe.

Promise's NLP engine is trained to recognise the subtle linguistic signals that separate a genuine commitment from a polite deflection — in real time, in natural conversation.

✓
Modal Verb Strength
"Will" vs "might" vs "could" vs "try" — each carries a different weight of obligation.
✓
Temporal Specificity
A named deadline ("by Thursday") vs a vague timeframe ("this week") vs no timeframe at all.
✓
Deliverable Clarity
A specific, named output vs an implied action vs a vague gesture of intent.
✓
Hedging Language Detection
"Hopefully," "if I can," "I'll try" — the phrases that signal a commitment is at risk.

FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot.

Transparency is one of our core values. Here are honest answers to the questions that matter most.

Is it legal to use Promise to record conversations? ▼
This depends on your jurisdiction. In many places, one-party consent is sufficient — meaning you can record a conversation you are part of. In others (including 11 U.S. states and several countries), all parties must consent. Promise provides in-app guidance and suggested disclosure language to help you stay compliant. We strongly recommend informing the other party that you are using an accountability app. Promise is not a covert surveillance tool — it is a transparency tool.
Does Promise actually listen to my conversations all the time? ▼
Absolutely not. Promise only activates when you explicitly tap the button to start a session. There is no passive or always-on listening mode. When you end the session, the microphone access is immediately released. You are always in complete control of when Promise is active.
Does the other person need to have Promise installed? ▼
No. Promise is a single-user tool by design. Only you need the app. The nudges are private — only you see them. The Promise Ledger is your personal record. The other person does not need to do anything differently.
How does Promise protect my privacy? ▼
All speech recognition and commitment detection runs on your device — your audio and transcripts never leave your phone. The only data that syncs to our encrypted cloud servers is the structured commitment data in your Ledger (e.g., "Call back Marcus by Friday") — not the raw audio or full transcript. We will never sell your data. Our business model is subscriptions, which means our incentives are aligned with your interests, not with monetising your personal information.
What platforms does Promise work with? ▼
Promise works across phone calls (via iOS CallKit and Android Telecom API), video calls (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams), and in-person conversations via your device's microphone. We are continuously adding new integrations based on user demand.
How accurate is the commitment detection? ▼
Our NLP model achieves over 91% accuracy on our benchmark dataset of annotated conversational transcripts. False positives (flagging something as a commitment when it isn't) are rare and can be dismissed with a single tap. False negatives (missing a genuine commitment) are addressed by our continuous model training programme. You can also manually add commitments to your Ledger at any time.
